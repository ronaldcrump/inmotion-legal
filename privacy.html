Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026 · the same text shown in the app under Settings › About & Legal.

Who we are

In Motion is a recovery and physical-therapy exercise app. This policy explains what we collect when you use it, why, and the choices you have. Questions go to inmotionn27@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what the app needs to work for you:

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored as a one-way hash by our authentication provider), or, if you use Sign in with Apple or Google, the identifier and the name and email those services share with us.
  • Profile: your first name and, if you add it, your last name; the answers you give when you set up your account (why you are here, the body areas you want to focus on, how hard sessions should feel, your activity level, and how often and how long you plan to train); and, if you choose to add them, height and weight. Appearance settings (theme, colour) are stored with your profile so they follow you across devices.
  • Activity you log: routines and workouts, sets, reps and time, completed sessions, daily notes, soreness or pain levels on a 0 to 10 scale with optional notes, and the insights you favourite.
  • Care (optional): if you join a practice with an invite code, the sessions, soreness check-ins and messages you record inside the Care tab are shared with that practice, along with your name. Nothing you do outside the Care tab is ever visible to a practice.
  • Feedback you send through the app, including any contact details you choose to include.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability, such as IP address and request timestamps.

What we do not collect

In Motion contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not read your contacts, photos, location, or Apple Health data, and we do not use identifiers for advertising.

Health-related information

Soreness and pain levels, height, weight, exercise history, and the recovery goal and focus areas you tell us about are health-related. We use them only to run the app for you: to show your history and insights, and, if you have joined a practice through Care, to show that practice the activity you record in the Care tab. We never use health-related information for advertising or marketing, never sell it, and never share it with anyone other than a practice you have chosen to connect with.

How we use your information

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on the contract we have with you (running the app), your consent (sharing through Care, which you can withdraw at any time), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

  • To provide the app: keep your account, sync your routines, history and settings across your devices, and show your insights.
  • To run the Care feature you opted into, so a practice you joined can see what you record in the Care tab.
  • To respond to feedback and support requests.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent abuse.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We would disclose information only if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

  • Supabase, our hosting and database provider, which stores your data on servers in the United States (us-east-1) under industry-standard security controls.
  • Apple and Google, only to sign you in when you choose Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • A practice you connect with through Care, limited to the Care-tab activity described above, for as long as you stay connected.

Care: your controls

  • Sharing soreness check-ins with a practice is a switch in Care settings, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • You can leave a practice whenever you like. After leaving you can remove the sessions, check-ins and messages you shared, from Care settings.
  • The practice can see who on its team viewed your Care record; you can see that access log too.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account (Settings › Delete Account) permanently removes your account and everything tied to it, including your profile, routines, history, notes, soreness logs, feedback and anything you shared through Care, including a practice’s records that reference you. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. Backups held by our hosting provider expire on their own schedule, typically within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, export your data by asking us, delete your account in Settings, and withdraw Care sharing in Care settings. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, portability, objection, or lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us and we will help.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored encrypted at rest. Every database row is protected by row-level security, so an account can only ever read its own data and the Care records it is part of. On your device the sign-in session is stored encrypted, with the key held in the device keychain.

Children

In Motion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will show the new version in the app and update the effective date. Continuing to use In Motion after that means you accept the updated policy.
