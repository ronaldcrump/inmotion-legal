Recovery, one session at a time.

In Motion is a recovery and physical-therapy exercise app for iPhone.

Follow guided routines with video demos and hold timers, log every set and side, and track soreness and progress over time. Physical therapists can share home programs with their clients and see how the week is going.

In Motion is currently in beta on TestFlight.
