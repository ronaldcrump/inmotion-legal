Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · the same text shown in the app under Settings › About & Legal.

What this policy covers

Some of what you tell In Motion can say something about your health. Privacy laws in Washington, Nevada and some other states call this consumer health data and give you extra rights over it. This policy explains exactly what health information we collect, why, where it comes from, who receives it and how to use your rights. It applies to everyone who uses In Motion, wherever you live, alongside the Privacy Policy.

What we collect and why

  • Your answers to the health questions in account setup, only if you agree to them: why you are here (for example, getting moving again after an injury), the body areas you want to focus on, and how hard sessions should feel. We keep them in your Profile so you can see and change them, and may use them to show routines for your areas first when you browse. They never assign you a routine or plan.
  • Soreness or pain levels (0 to 10) and notes you log, and daily notes. Used to show your history, calendar and insights.
  • Your workouts: the routines and plans you choose (a routine’s name can point to a condition, such as tennis elbow), the sets, reps and time you log, and the sessions you finish. Used to run your sessions, keep your history and show your progress.
  • Height and weight, only if you add them. Shown in your Profile; nothing in the app calculates with them.
  • What you record in the Care tab, only if you join a practice: sessions, soreness check-ins and messages. Used to share them with that practice.
  • Feedback you write, which may mention your health. Used to answer you and improve the app.
  • Usage data, only if you turn it on: which screens and routines you open, start or leave early. Used to see which parts of the app are used, as totals.

Where it comes from

  • You, when you answer questions, log sessions and soreness, write notes or send feedback.
  • Your device, for usage data if you turn it on: the app and operating-system version.
  • A practice you choose to join through Care: the programs, notes and messages they record for you.

Who receives it

That is the complete list. We do not sell health information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers, advertisers or anyone else. We do not use location or geofences. We would disclose information only if the law required it.

  • Supabase, our hosting and database provider, which stores all of it for us on servers in the United States and may use it only to provide that service to us.
  • A practice you choose to join through Care, only the activity you record in the Care tab, only while you stay connected, and only with your consent, which you give by joining and can withdraw by leaving.

Your consent

We ask before the health questions in account setup, and you can skip them and still use every routine. Usage data is off unless you turn it on. Sharing with a practice happens only if you join one. Logging soreness, notes and workouts happens only when you choose to log them.

You can withdraw consent at any time: clear your answers in Profile, turn off Settings › Share usage data (which also deletes what was sent), leave a practice in Care settings, delete individual logs, or delete your account.

Your rights

Email inmotionn27@gmail.com from the address on your account. We answer within 45 days, free of charge, and tell you if we need up to 45 more days. If we decline, we will say why, and you can appeal by replying with "appeal"; we answer appeals within 45 days. If we turn down your appeal, you can complain to your state attorney general (in Washington, through the Attorney General's online complaint form).

  • Confirm whether we collect or share your health information, and see it: most of it is in the app already, and we will send you a copy on request.
  • Get the list of everyone we have shared it with (above, and any practice you joined).
  • Withdraw your consent, as described above.
  • Delete it: Settings › Delete Account removes everything immediately, or ask us to delete particular data. Backups held by our hosting provider expire within about 30 days.

Security and keeping it

Health information travels encrypted, is stored encrypted, and is protected by row-level security so each account can only read its own data and the Care records it is part of. Only our team can reach the database, and only to run and support the app. We keep it while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account.

Changes and contact

If we change what health information we collect, why, or who receives it, we will update this policy and ask for your consent again before doing anything new with it. Questions: inmotionn27@gmail.com.
